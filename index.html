Dermis is building tactile skins that help robotic hands and grippers feel where they make contact, how much force they apply, and when an object starts to slip.

Why touch

Vision tells a robot where an object is. Touch tells it where contact happens, how firmly it is holding, and when the object starts to shift.

Signals Dermis is developing

  • ForceHow hard the object presses
  • ShearSideways drag across the pad
  • GripHolding force between fingers
  • SlipWhen an object starts to move
  • TemperatureWarmth at the contact surface
  • Contact locationWhere on the pad touch happens
